`scripts/gen-ping.py` (python3 stdlib only: `wave`, `math`, `struct`, `argparse`; run with `python3 -I`): synthesize a deterministic, original "ping": 44100 Hz, mono, 16-bit PCM, total length 0.45 s. Signal = partial 1 at 1046.5 Hz (C6) amplitude 0.6 + partial 2 at 1568 Hz (G6) amplitude 0.3 + partial 3 at 2093 Hz amplitude 0.08, each with a 4 ms linear attack and exponential decay (tau 0.11 s, 0.07 s, 0.04 s), a slight upward pitch glide of +1.5% over the first 60 ms on partial 1, and a 20 ms cosine fade-out at the end; normalize peak to 0.7 (about -3 dBFS); no randomness. Write to `src-tauri/resources/sounds/mornfold-ping.wav` (create directories). `--check PATH` mode validates: PCM, 16-bit, mono, 44100 Hz, duration < 1.0 s, peak <= 0.75, and exits non-zero otherwise. `scripts/gen-icons.py` (python3 stdlib only: `zlib`, `struct`, `math`, `argparse`): a tiny rasterizer with 4×4 supersampling and a PNG RGBA writer (signature, IHDR, IDAT via zlib level 9, IEND, CRC via `zlib.crc32`).
